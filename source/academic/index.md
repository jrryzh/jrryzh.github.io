## Open-Source Contributions
- **InternManip** — Contributor & Maintainer  
  A unified framework for robotic training and evaluation.  
  [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/InternRobotics/InternManip)

- **InternData-M1 dataset** — Core Contributor  
  A large-scale embodied robotics dataset (~250k simulated demonstrations) with rich annotations, including 2D/3D boxes, trajectories, grasp points, and semantic masks.  
  [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/InternRobotics/InternData-M1)

- **InternVLA-M1** — Core Contributor  
  A Spatially Grounded Foundation Model for Generalist Robot Policy. 
  [GitHub Repo](https://github.com/InternRobotics/InternVLA-M1)

- **IROS 2025 Challenge Organization** — Contributor & Maintainer    
  Core contributor to the [IROS 2025 Challenge on Dual-Arm Manipulation](https://internrobotics.shlab.org.cn/challenge/2025/), featuring 10 carefully designed scenarios with diverse tasks that assess multi-scale robotic capabilities.
  The dataset is available on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/InternRobotics/IROS-2025-Challenge-Manip).
